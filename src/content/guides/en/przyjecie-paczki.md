I ship animals so that they spend as little time in transit as possible, with heat or cool packs depending on the weather (details: [shipping](/en/shipping/)). A few simple steps after delivery will get them into their new box in good shape.

## Before delivery

- Prepare the enclosure with substrate, hides and heating **before** the parcel arrives.
- If possible, be at home on delivery day – the parcel should not sit in a mailbox, in frost or in the sun.

## In winter

1. Collect the parcel as soon as possible and **do not open it straight away in a warm room** if it has been out in the cold – leave it closed for 30–60 minutes at room temperature to even out.
2. The heat pack inside may still be warm – that is normal.
3. Only then open it and move the animals.

## In summer

1. Do not leave the parcel in a hot car or in the sun.
2. Open it in a cooler part of your home.

## Unpacking

- Open the box **over the destination enclosure** – small nymphs can run fast.
- Tip the contents in together with the transport material (egg crates, substrate), wait for the animals to hide, and only then remove the packaging.
- On the first day offer a piece of vegetable (water) and dry food.
- The animals may look sluggish for a day or two – normal after shipping.

## If something went wrong

Live arrival is guaranteed. If there are dead animals in the parcel, **take a photo of the contents on the day of delivery** (ideally before moving them) and send it by [e-mail](mailto:mikolaj.stachowiak@blattodea.pl) – we will sort it out.
